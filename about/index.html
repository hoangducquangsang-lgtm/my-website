The materials behind the story

Our core range brings together coffee wood, coconut fiber, hemp fiber and loofah. The supplied WINVN company material describes natural pet-product manufacturing experience since 2018. Coffee wood is linked to Vietnam's Central Highlands, with preparation and finishing that turn mature timber into a defined product format.

The next step for a buyer is practical: evaluate the sample, check who produces it, review the construction and confirm what will appear on the invoice and shipment documents.
