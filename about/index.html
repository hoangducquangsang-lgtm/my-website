About VietPaw

A Vietnamese manufacturer and exporter of natural-material pet toys — how we produce, what we will not claim, and where you can come and look.

Who VietPaw is

VietPaw is a Vietnamese manufacturer and exporter of natural-material pet toys. We make coffee wood chews, coconut fiber balls, hemp rope toys and loofah cat shapes ourselves, in our own three factories in Dak Lak, Gia Lai and Binh Duong, hold standard sizes in our warehouse in Ho Chi Minh City, and ship on our own export documents. Orders go from our stock or our line straight to your container — there is no trading layer in between.

That matters for three practical reasons. Stock sizes ship in 5–7 days because they are already packed, not because we are chasing another factory. Quality decisions — what counts as a rejectable crack, what moisture we pack at — are ours to make and ours to change for your order. And when you want to verify something, you are asking the people who ran the line rather than a middleman relaying your question.

What we will not do is overclaim. Most of what gets printed in this category — splinter-free, dental benefits, certified biodegradable — has no evidence behind it, and a buyer who prints it inherits the risk. We would rather lose a sale than hand you a claim that fails.

Our three factories

We manufacture everything we sell ourselves, in three factories we operate in Vietnam: Dak Lak and Gia Lai in the Central Highlands, close to where the coffee wood is collected, and Binh Duong in the south. Packing, quality release and export documentation run from our Ho Chi Minh City office and warehouse.

Buyer-arranged visits and third-party inspection are welcome at any site; please arrange in advance so the line is running when you arrive. Loading port is Cat Lai, Ho Chi Minh City.

What we do and do not do

We do this

  • Specifying a product properly before the first order, including the components a photograph does not show.
  • Wholesale from approved standard specifications across four materials.
  • Private label — engraving from 50 pcs, printed packaging from 500 pcs per SKU.
  • OEM/ODM development from a drawing or brief, through feasibility and prototype.
  • Export documentation, carton planning and the moisture records that come with a wood shipment.

We do not do this

  • We do not claim certifications we do not hold, or present planning figures as audited output.
  • We do not publish biodegradability, dental-health, digestibility or “splinter-free” claims.
  • We do not quote a chew lifespan in days or weeks; the dog sets that, not the material.

The materials, and where they come from

Coffee wood comes from mature Robusta stems in Vietnam’s Central Highlands — Dak Lak, Gia Lai and Dak Nong. Growers take out the trees that have stopped paying their way and leave the rest alone, so these stems already existed and previously went to firewood; nobody fells a coffee tree that is still yielding cherries. Stems are cut to billets of roughly 50 to 70 cm in the field, then held about a year in ventilated shade before anything is machined.

Coconut fiber is coir from the husk, itself a by-product of the coconut food and oil trade. Loofah is the dried fibrous interior of a gourd, grown on vines and peeled by hand. Hemp is a bast fiber spun into cord.

Two of those four — coffee wood and coconut husk — have a genuine material-reuse story we are happy to put in writing. Hemp and loofah are plant-derived but are grown as crops, and we do not describe them as agricultural waste.

How we handle evidence

  1. We separate the reference from the record
    Action: Everything published here is a reference specification; the record is the moisture reading, the grading result and the carton marks filed against your lot.
    Result: You check the record, not the brochure.
  2. We say when a figure is internally reported
    Action: Reject rate and process figures come from our own production records, not audited measurements.
    Result: You can weight them accordingly instead of treating them as verified.
  3. We say when nothing exists
    Action: There is no hardness figure for coffee wood, no tensile rating for these rope toys and no published chew lifespan.
    Result: You do not build a listing on a number that was invented for marketing.
  4. We support independent verification
    Action: Third-party inspection can witness the moisture reading, measure a sample, photograph carton marks against the packing list and witness stuffing.
    Result: Evidence that does not depend on trusting us.

Buyer questions

Does VietPaw manufacture its own products?

Yes. We manufacture ourselves, in our own three factories in Dak Lak, Gia Lai and Binh Duong, with a production capacity of 100,000 pieces a month. There is no trading layer: the factory that makes your order is ours, and you are welcome to visit or send an inspector.

How long have you been exporting?

The company has been registered in Vietnam since 12 November 2019 and now ships to 40+ countries. Country count is our own record rather than an audited figure, and we present it as such.

Can I visit our factory or send an inspector?

Yes. Buyer-arranged inspection is welcome, and for a shipment that matters we would encourage it: a third party can witness the moisture reading, measure a sample, photograph carton marks against the packing list and witness stuffing before the container is sealed.

Why does the site carry so many caveats?

Because the alternative is a listing you cannot defend. Most of what gets overclaimed in this category — splinter-free, dental benefits, biodegradability, certified pet-safe — has no evidence behind it, and a buyer who prints it inherits the risk. We would rather lose a sale than hand you a claim that fails.

Do you sell direct to consumers?

No. VietPaw is business-to-business: wholesale, private label and OEM/ODM for brands, distributors, retailers and marketplace sellers.
