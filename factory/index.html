Two factories, one specification

Coffee wood is produced at our two factories in Vietnam — one in Dak Lak and one in Gia Lai, both in the Central Highlands close to where the wood is collected. Both build to our specification and release against our quality checks. Goods are shipped from our warehouse in Ho Chi Minh City, where packing, quality release and export documents are prepared.

Coffee wood drying and quality protocol

Coffee wood follows a drying and quality protocol with five QC checkpoints. The five checkpoints are inspection controls within that process.

Coffee wood moisture is checked on every batch: below 14% before packing at the factory, then the pieces are vacuum packed with a desiccant sachet. Batch moisture readings are available on request.

We keep its operating parameters confidential; the published figure is the finished-batch moisture threshold, not the drying recipe. Ask for the moisture record linked to your order. Drying temperatures, cycle times and the stage sequence are not published.

From plantation to billet: raw material intake

Coffee wood reaches our factories as stems taken out during a grower’s normal replanting cycle in Dak Lak and Gia Lai. They are cut to billets in the field, delivered to the intake yard and stacked in the open for about a year before machining.

Drying, grading and the moisture record

After shaping, chews are dried and graded before they are packed. Batch moisture is measured below 14% and the reading is filed against your lot.

Packing: vacuum pack, desiccant, export carton

Standard coffee wood is vacuum packed with a desiccant sachet inside, layered into the export master carton and labelled by size and quantity.

Ho Chi Minh City: warehouse, inspection and loading

Goods are shipped from our warehouse in Ho Chi Minh City. Stock sizes are held there, pre-shipment inspection can be arranged before the container is sealed, and the container leaves for Cat Lai port.

What to request in a factory verification pack

  • Current company registration and site details matching the proposed contract.
  • A dated walkthrough identifying the product line and stages handled there.
  • Production slot and capacity calculation for the actual SKU mix and pack.
  • Quality checkpoints, measuring equipment and sample/lot identification.
  • Arrangements for independent pre-shipment inspection at the packing warehouse.
  • Authorized, appropriately redacted shipment examples where relevant.

Review the quality-control workflow and document scope before agreeing the inspection brief.

Buyer questions

How is production capacity confirmed?

Monthly capacity is 100,000 pieces across the product lines. That figure is a ceiling, not a promise for your order: capacity is planned against your actual product mix and packaging, and the lead time and production slot are confirmed in your quotation.

Where is production carried out?

Coffee wood is produced at our two factories, in Dak Lak and Gia Lai. The specification, the production plan, the QC checkpoints and the quality release are ours, and so are the export documents. Goods are shipped from our Ho Chi Minh City warehouse, and we will name the site that makes your order.

How can I verify production without travelling?

Ask for dated photos of your line, a video walkthrough and the batch moisture record filed against your lot. Pre-shipment inspection can be arranged at our Ho Chi Minh City warehouse before the container is sealed.
