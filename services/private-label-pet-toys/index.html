Private label is suited to buyers who want a branded range without beginning with a new product construction. A changed shape or assembly may move the brief into OEM/ODM development.

A pack brief that prevents avoidable revisions

  • Brand name, logo file, approved colors and contact information for the relevant market.
  • Product name, complete material description, size and intended use.
  • Supervision and replacement instructions appropriate to the product.
  • Barcode artwork from the buyer and clear unit/set/carton identification.
  • Any environmental or performance claim with evidence and scope.

A natural-material toy in a plastic film is not a plastic-free pack. Confirm coatings, inks, glue and wrapping before approving final sustainability language.

Minimums and commercial scope

Selected standard products can start from 50 pcs per SKU, but this does not mean every printing or packaging option has the same minimum. WINVN's service information describes custom box quantities around 200 pieces per design; the current specification and quote decide the applicable minimum.

Ask the quote to distinguish product cost, engraving, label printing, box printing, artwork support, media and freight. Do not assume customization is free.

Amazon preparation · Eco-retail packaging · Startup launch planning

Approval and reorder control

  1. Approve the unbranded product and size.
  2. Approve a branded physical sample and packaging proof.
  3. Record the approved artwork version and component list.
  4. Confirm quote, lead time, inspection and shipping requirements.
  5. Reorder against the same reference, with changes approved before production.

Review factory and production information, the five-stage QC workflow and testing and export-document scope before approving your order.

Buyer questions

Can I use my own logo?

Yes. Engraving is for suitable wood surfaces; fiber toys usually use tags, labels or branded packaging.

Is private label the same as exclusivity?

No. Brand presentation does not automatically make an existing product design exclusive. Discuss any exclusivity separately.

Can I start with a single SKU?

Yes, discuss the standard-product minimum and the packaging minimum separately.
