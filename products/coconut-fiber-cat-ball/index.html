Sizes, formats and private-label options

  • Choose the diameter and finished weight for the cat range; no dog-size chart is reused here.
  • Approve winding, surface texture and any internal or binding components.
  • Use a branded tag or small paper box; define whether units are sold singly or as a set.

For branding an existing item, see private-label pet toys. For structural changes, use our OEM/ODM development service.

Quality control and use instructions

  • Check for loose strands and attachment security before packing.
  • Compare sample diameter, mass and construction across the order.
  • Reject musty or visibly contaminated units and define clean, dry storage conditions.

For supervised pet play only, not food. Select a size that cannot be swallowed whole. Remove damaged toys, loose strands or pieces, and replace worn items. Hard chews can damage teeth; seek veterinary advice for puppies, dental conditions or forceful chewing.

Review factory and production information, the five-stage QC workflow and testing and export-document scope before approving your order.
