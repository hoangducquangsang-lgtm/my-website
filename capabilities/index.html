  • Logo & labelling — your brand, front and centre

How a private-label project runs

  1. Tell us your idea — product, materials, brand direction
  2. Free sample & prototype — approve the real thing before you buy
  3. Confirm the run — sizes, packaging, engraving, quantities
  4. Production — 25–30 working days for OEM/ODM orders
  5. Export & delivery — full documentation, worldwide shipping

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order for private label?

From 50 pcs per SKU. We keep it low so you can pilot a product before scaling to bulk.

How fast can I get a branded sample?

Samples are typically ready within about 7 days, and prototypes for custom designs within a few days of confirming the brief.

Do you charge extra for OEM service?

No extra service fees apply when you use our standard packaging. Custom packaging and special finishes are quoted per project.

Which products can be private-labelled?

All of our natural lines — coffee wood chews, coconut fiber, hemp fiber and loofah toys.
