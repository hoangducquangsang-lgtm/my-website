What can be customized

  • Logo engraving on suitable wood surfaces, with position and legibility agreed on the sample.
  • Product tags, size labels, paper/kraft boxes and pack artwork.
  • Wood-and-rope combinations, rope geometry and loofah shapes, subject to feasibility.
  • Product photography or video support by agreement; scope and cost depend on the project.

WINVN INT CO., LTD describes these services in its official manufacturing and order information. VietPaw is the commercial/export brand. Specific charges, minimums and deliverables are confirmed in your quotation.

Before approving a sample

  1. Agree materials, dimensions, tolerances and intended pet/use.
  2. List every component, including thread, cores, adhesive and packaging film.
  3. Confirm artwork, barcode file, warnings and market-specific testing needs.
  4. Record approval and the circumstances that require a new sample.

Review factory and production information, the coffee wood six-stage drying/quality protocol with five QC checkpoints, testing and export-document scope and the Proof document register before approving your order.

Buyer questions

Is every customization included in the standard price?

No. Engraving, printing, box production, new tooling, tests and media work must be itemized where applicable.

Does a small product MOQ also apply to printed boxes?

No. Selected standard products and suitable coffee wood engraving start at 50 pcs. Private-label runs start at 500 pcs. Custom hang tags, labels and printed boxes start at 500 pcs; confirm the quantity per SKU and artwork in your quote.
