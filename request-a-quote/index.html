What happens next

We review your brief and confirm product availability, MOQ, sample terms and an indicative timeline. New development or testing may need a separate feasibility discussion.

  • Production lead time: 5–7 days for orders under 500 pcs; 60–80 days for a full container. Orders of 500 pcs or more below container volume, mixed orders and custom development need a project-specific schedule. Confirm the production start date after sample, artwork and order approval. Development, testing and freight are additional; production time is not an arrival date.
