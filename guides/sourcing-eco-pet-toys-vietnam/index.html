Sourcing Natural Pet Toys from Vietnam: The First Order

By Sarah · VietPaw · Updated 23 July 2026

Short answer: a first order of natural pet toys from Vietnam runs in six stages — brief, samples, quote, approval, production and shipment. With VietPaw, stock-packed goods take 5–7 days to produce and 30–35 days by sea to the EU or the US; orders with your own branding take 60–80 days to produce. Start with 3 free samples, then a small stock-packed order from 50 pcs per SKU.

What sourcing natural pet toys from Vietnam involves

Sourcing here means buying finished pet toys directly from a Vietnamese manufacturer and importing them under your own name — as a brand, retailer, distributor or marketplace seller. Vietnam’s natural-material base is the reason buyers look here: coffee wood is collected in the Central Highlands, and coconut and loofah are grown in the country.

VietPaw manufactures across four material collections — coffee wood, coconut fiber, hemp and loofah — in three factories in Dak Lak, Gia Lai and Binh Duong. Packing, quality release and export documents run from our Ho Chi Minh City office and warehouse, and goods leave from Cat Lai port.

Most of the useful work on a first order happens before the purchase order: narrowing the assortment, approving a sample and deciding what must be ready before the goods leave Vietnam.

The first order, step by step

  1. Send a brief we can price
    Action: Destination, sales channel, product references, sizes and quantity per SKU, and whether you want stock goods, your logo or a changed construction.
    Result: An itemised quote rather than a price list.
  2. Approve samples
    Action: Up to 3 free samples, dispatched within 1 working day once the selection and courier are confirmed.
    Result: A physical reference with written dimensions.
  3. Agree the terms
    Action: Incoterm with a named place, payment, document list and inspection stage.
    Result: No gaps about who pays for what, or when.
  4. Approve artwork (branded orders)
    Action: Logo, barcode, warnings and pack contents on a proof.
    Result: Locked print files; the production clock can start.
  5. Release production
    Action: 30% deposit for stock, 50% for a branded order.
    Result: 5–7 days stock; 60–80 days branded.
  6. Inspect and ship
    Action: Final check, carton marks, seal number and the seven export documents.
    Result: Balance paid against documents; 30–35 days sea transit.
  7. Receive and record
    Action: Count, inspect against the sample and log any issue with the batch reference.
    Result: A receiving report that improves the repeat order.

We do not issue veterinary certificates — coffee wood is a wood article, not an animal by-product — or FSC certification, since coffee is an agricultural crop outside its scope. Laboratory testing is arranged separately on request. Have your forwarder or broker confirm the list for your destination before dispatch.

Approve the retail pack as carefully as the toy

Request a sample of the proposed pack, including label placement, barcode area and any protective wrapping. Check how the product sits inside it and whether the important instructions remain readable. A product sample in a plain courier bag does not approve a future printed retail box.

Keep the sample reference, agreed dimensions and artwork version together, and name who can approve a change. This avoids a familiar problem: a factory waiting for artwork while the buyer believes the production clock has already started.

Build quality checks into the order

Agree the defects that matter for the construction, the inspection stage and what happens to non-conforming goods. For wood, discuss dimensions, finish, cracks and drying records. For fiber assemblies, include winding, knots and attachments. Add pack counts, labels and carton marks to the same inspection plan.

A photograph from a previous order can show a process. It cannot replace inspection information for the lot you are purchasing. Your order needs an identifiable reference from sample approval through receiving.

Frequently asked

How long does a first order from Vietnam take?

With VietPaw, about 35–42 days from production start to an EU or US port for stock-packed goods (5–7 days production plus 30–35 days sea transit), or about 90–115 days with your own branding. Sample courier time, booking and clearance are extra.

What is the smallest first order?

Selected standard products start from 50 pcs per SKU. For coffee wood chews, the usual first steps are a Trial Box of 100 pcs or a Starting Box of 500 pcs; private label starts at 500 pcs per SKU.

Are samples free?

Yes, 3 free samples. You cover the courier, and standard samples are dispatched within 1 working day once the selection and courier arrangements are confirmed.

Which documents come with the shipment?

Seven: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading or Air Waybill, Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary Certificate, Fumigation Certificate and a Forest-Product Declaration. Batch moisture readings are available on request.

Which port do goods ship from?

Cat Lai, Ho Chi Minh City. Sea transit to the EU or the US is commonly quoted at 30–35 days port to port.

Can I visit the factory before ordering?

Yes. Buyer visits and third-party inspection are welcome at any of our three factories; arrange in advance so the line is running when you arrive.
