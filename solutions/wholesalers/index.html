What your sourcing plan needs to address

Reorder consistency

Use the approved sample, material list, size bands and pack configuration for repeat orders.

Quantity-based pricing

Request price breaks relevant to your SKU mix instead of applying one universal tier table.

Supply planning

Share demand by SKU, required dispatch dates and seasonal peaks.

Receiving checks

Agree carton labels, pack counts and inspection/claim terms before shipment.

Make the decision on the right evidence

The supplied company material quotes indicative annual capacity, but that does not reserve a production slot. Ask for a plan against your product mix and schedule. Territory or product exclusivity is a separate negotiation and should never be inferred from a volume order.

Manufacturer and quality checks

Review factory and production information, the coffee wood six-stage drying/quality protocol with five QC checkpoints and testing and export-document scope before approving your order.

Confirm report scope, required inspections and the written handling of non-conforming goods. A process description does not guarantee every future order or market approval.

Buyer questions

Can I request a sample before deciding the range?

Yes. Tell us which product, size and pack you want to evaluate. Availability, sample terms and courier costs are confirmed first.

What should I send for a useful quote?

Send quantities per SKU, destination, required timing and the packaging or development brief. Attach the applicable retailer or marketplace requirements if available.
